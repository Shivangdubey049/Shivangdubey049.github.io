★ FEATURED PROJECT

OracleShield

An AI network-security prototype that forecasts where an attack will move next. Most intrusion detection asks what attack is happening now; OracleShield uses an LSTM world model over evolving network states to look ahead.

Traffic→State S_t→RandomForest + LSTM→Forward rollout→MITRE ATT&CK stage→SHA-256 audit chain
  • Predicts the next network state and attack-stage distribution
  • Persistent threat memory for recurring attacker trajectories
  • Tamper-evident hash-chained audit ledger with a live tamper demo
  • Trained on NSL-KDD (148,517 records) and reported with macro precision, recall and F1
